# Adopt the omakase foundation with resources beside their owner

The 96-entry count below is extended to 97 by the accepted `nh-frontend-setup` addition in [ADR 0005](0005-bootstrap-frontends-through-the-feature-pipeline.md). The other collection and resource-ownership decisions remain in force.

The agreed catalog preserves the 27 existing entries, incorporates the 54 shared and 11 adaptable skills from the researched inventory, excludes its six framework-internal skills, and adds `nh-app-context`, `nh-api-client`, `nh-frontend`, and `nh-skills`: 96 entries installed together. Nick adopts the omakase philosophy without reopening its individual principles. Additions use `nh-`; the 27 historical entries retain their names. Every resource belongs to a distributable skill under `skills/<name>/`, with relative sibling references and conditional reading from entry files, so use requires no framework checkout.

Git records content and history. Nick rejected an additional inventory or manifest and separate tracking of origin, revision, license, dependencies, or divergence; this supersedes the earlier provenance-record requirement. Responsibilities and references express ownership. The event schema belongs to `event-modeling`, roles to `nh-roles`, and collection maintenance to `nh-skills`. Execution receipts stay outside reusable knowledge. Preserve applicable original notices with their resources. The [catalog decision agreed with Nick](https://plane.p.egregore.io/egregore/projects/5535680e-e3f6-4023-b400-2b27544130e2/issues/938cd5dd-733b-449e-b7d8-689eb918ea8c/) retains the full contract. This ADR records planning; it is not implementation evidence.
